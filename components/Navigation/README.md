# Navigation

The sidebar: the wordmark and descriptor, then mono labels with their ⌘ shortcuts.

- The wordmark is set in the `mark` face (Cutive Mono); the item labels stay in label mono.
- The sidebar sits on `raised`, divided from the content by one full `rule`.
- The active item shifts to the instrument's `*-selection` ground and its label turns `ink`. Nothing else: no left bar, no accent text, no rounded tint.
- Inactive labels and their shortcuts are `label`. On the active row the shortcut turns `ink-soft`, because `label` drops to about 4:1 on a selection ground.

The consumer provides the items, their shortcuts and which one is active.
